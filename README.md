# 🛍️ Consumer Behavior Analytics
 
> **Leveraging retail shopping data to uncover trends, strengthen customer engagement, and optimise marketing and product strategies.**

---
 
## 📖 Project Overview
 
This project analyses a retail company's consumer behaviour dataset to help the business make smarter, data-driven decisions. It covers the full analytics pipeline — from raw data cleaning through to an interactive Power BI dashboard and a stakeholder-ready report.
 
The project is structured to be accessible to **both technical practitioners** (who want to understand the code and methodology) and **non-technical stakeholders** (who want to understand what the findings mean for the business).
 
---
 
## 💼 Business Problem
 
A leading retail company has observed notable shifts in how customers shop — across demographics, product categories, and channels (online vs. in-store). The management team needs to understand:
 
- What is driving changes in purchasing patterns?
- Which customer segments are most valuable — and which are at risk?
- Are discounts and promotions actually building loyalty, or just attracting one-time buyers?
- How do seasonal trends, payment preferences, and product reviews influence buying decisions?
Without answers to these questions, the business risks misallocating marketing budgets, stocking the wrong products, and losing customers to more data-savvy competitors.

## 📦 Deliverables
 
### 1. 🐍 Data Preparation (Python)
Cleans and transforms the raw dataset — resolving inconsistencies, filling or flagging missing values, correcting data types, and engineering features that are essential for meaningful analysis.
 
### 2. 🗄️ Data Analysis (SQL)
Organises cleaned data into a structured relational database. SQL queries extract insights on customer segments, loyalty behaviour, purchase drivers, and channel performance.
 
### 3. 📊 Interactive Dashboard (Power BI)
A fully filterable dashboard allowing stakeholders to explore trends by region, product category, time period, and customer segment — no technical knowledge required.
 
### 4. 📄 Report & Presentation
A written project report summarising methodology, findings, and recommendations. Accompanied by an executive presentation designed for stakeholder briefings.
 
### 5. 💻 GitHub Repository *(this repo)*
All code, queries, and files stored in a well-structured, version-controlled repository — documented for reproducibility and future extension.

 ## ✅ Business Recommendations
 
> *Full recommendations are detailed in `reports/project_report.pdf`. Summary below.*
 
1. **Prioritise retention over acquisition** for high-frequency customer segments — they cost less to retain and deliver more lifetime value.
2. **Redesign discount strategy** — shift from blanket promotions to loyalty-tiered rewards that incentivise repeat behaviour rather than one-off purchases.
3. **Invest in high-growth channels** — allocate incremental marketing spend toward the channels showing the strongest growth trajectory.
4. **Build seasonal campaign calendars** — align inventory and promotions with identified demand peaks by category, at least 6–8 weeks in advance.
5. **Leverage review data** — surface high-rated products more prominently in digital channels and use review sentiment to guide product development priorities.
